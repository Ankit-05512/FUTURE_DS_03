# FUTURE_DS_03 — Task 3: Online Retail Analysis Insights

## 1. Overall Dataset Analysis

* The dataset contains online retail transaction records with information about invoices, products, quantities, prices, customers, and transaction dates.
* Data cleaning was performed to improve the quality and consistency of the dataset before analysis.
* Duplicate records and records with missing customer information were handled during preprocessing.

## 2. Transaction Analysis

* The analysis shows variation in transaction activity across different dates.
* Transaction patterns can help identify periods of relatively higher and lower customer activity.
* Time-based analysis provides useful information for understanding changes in retail demand.

## 3. Sales and Revenue Analysis

* Revenue can be analyzed using quantity and unit price to understand the monetary contribution of transactions.
* Transactions with higher quantities or higher unit prices can contribute significantly to overall revenue.
* Examining revenue over time helps identify changes in sales performance.

## 4. Product Analysis

* Product-level analysis helps identify products that generate higher transaction quantities or revenue.
* Some products contribute more strongly to overall sales than others.
* Identifying frequently purchased products can support inventory and product-management decisions.

## 5. Customer Analysis

* Customer-level analysis helps identify differences in purchasing activity among customers.
* Customers with higher purchase frequency or spending can be examined to understand valuable customer segments.
* Customer transaction history can be used to understand purchasing patterns and repeat activity.

## 6. Quantity Analysis

* Quantity sold varies across products and transactions.
* High-quantity transactions can indicate products with stronger demand.
* Quantity analysis can support inventory planning and help reduce the risk of stock shortages.

## 7. Returns / Negative Quantity Transactions

* The dataset contains transactions with negative quantities.
* Negative quantities are associated with returned or cancelled transaction records and should be considered separately from normal sales transactions.
* Separating sales and return-related transactions provides a clearer view of actual sales performance.

## 8. Date and Time Analysis

* The `InvoiceDate` field was converted into a proper datetime format during data preparation.
* Date-based analysis can reveal changes in customer activity and sales over time.
* Daily, monthly, and time-of-day patterns can be used to support operational planning.

## 9. Data Cleaning Insights

* Duplicate records were removed during preprocessing.
* Records with missing `CustomerID` values were handled before customer-level analysis.
* Negative quantity transactions were identified for separate return analysis.
* Converting `InvoiceDate` into datetime format made time-based analysis possible.
* Cleaning the dataset before visualization improved the reliability of the analysis.

## 10. Business Takeaways

* Product-level sales analysis can help identify products with stronger demand.
* Customer-level analysis can help understand purchasing frequency and customer value.
* Return transactions should be monitored because they affect net sales performance.
* Time-based analysis can help businesses understand demand patterns.
