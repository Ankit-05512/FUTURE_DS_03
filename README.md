# FUTURE_DS_03 – Bank Marketing Campaign Analysis & Power BI Dashboard

## 📌 Internship Task

This project was completed as **Task 3** of the **Future Interns Data Science & Analytics Internship**.

The objective of this task was to analyze bank marketing campaign data, understand customer and campaign characteristics, evaluate campaign conversion/subscription outcomes, and develop an interactive Power BI dashboard to generate meaningful business insights.

---

## 🎯 Project Objectives

* Inspect and understand the marketing campaign dataset.
* Clean and prepare the data for analysis.
* Handle missing, duplicate, inconsistent, and invalid values.
* Perform Exploratory Data Analysis (EDA).
* Analyze customer demographics and banking characteristics.
* Analyze marketing campaign and contact performance.
* Identify factors associated with campaign conversion/subscription.
* Create meaningful KPIs and visualizations.
* Build an interactive Power BI dashboard.
* Generate actionable business insights and recommendations.

## 🧹 Data Cleaning & Preparation

The dataset was inspected and prepared before performing the final analysis.

The data preparation process included:

* Checking the dataset structure and column data types.
* Identifying missing values.
* Checking duplicate records.
* Checking inconsistent and invalid values.
* Converting fields to appropriate data types.
* Preparing numerical and categorical variables for analysis.
* Creating useful analytical fields where required.
* Preparing the cleaned dataset for Power BI visualization.

The final cleaned dataset was used for the EDA and dashboard development.

---

## 📊 Exploratory Data Analysis

The analysis focused on understanding customer characteristics, campaign performance, and subscription outcomes.

The major areas analyzed were:

* Customer age and demographics.
* Job and education characteristics.
* Marital status.
* Account balance.
* Housing and personal loans.
* Contact methods.
* Campaign contact frequency.
* Call/contact duration.
* Previous campaign contact.
* Monthly campaign activity.
* Final campaign outcome.

The target outcome field is:

* **`y = yes`** → Customer subscribed/converted.
* **`y = no`** → Customer did not subscribe/convert.

# 📈 Power BI Dashboard

The final analysis was presented through an interactive Power BI dashboard.

The dashboard is organized into four analytical pages.

---

## 1. Overview

The Overview page provides a high-level summary of the bank marketing campaign.

### KPIs

* Total Customers
* Subscribed Customers
* Subscription Rate
* Average Balance
* Average Call Duration

### Visualizations

* Subscription Outcome
* Subscription Rate by Job
* Subscription Rate by Education
* Monthly Subscription Trend

### Filters

* Job
* Education
* Month

---

## 2. Customer Analysis

This page focuses on customer characteristics and their relationship with campaign outcomes.

### KPIs

* Average Age
* Average Balance
* Housing Loan Customers
* Personal Loan Customers

### Visualizations

* Customers by Age Group
* Subscription Rate by Age Group
* Marital Status vs Subscription
* Subscription Rate by Balance Category
* Age vs Account Balance

---

## 3. Campaign & Contact Analysis

This page analyzes the effectiveness and characteristics of marketing contacts.

### KPIs

* Average Campaign Contacts
* Average Call Duration
* Previously Contacted Customers
* Average Previous Contacts

### Visualizations

* Subscription Rate by Contact Method
* Subscription Rate by Campaign Contacts
* Subscription Rate by Call Duration
* Monthly Campaign Performance
* Previous Contact vs Subscription Outcome

---

## 4. Subscription Analysis

This page provides a deeper analysis of the factors associated with campaign subscription.

### KPIs

* Subscribed Customers
* Subscription Rate
* Average Balance of Subscribers
* Average Duration of Subscribers
* Average Campaign Contacts of Subscribers

### Visualizations

* Subscription Rate by Job
* Housing Loan vs Subscription
* Personal Loan vs Subscription
* Subscription Rate by Contact Method
* Balance Category vs Subscription
* Campaign Category vs Subscription

---

## 💡 Business Insights

The analysis was used to identify:

* Customer groups with different subscription rates.
* Differences in campaign response across job and education groups.
* Relationship between contact method and subscription outcome.
* Relationship between campaign contact frequency and conversion.
* Relationship between call duration and subscription.
* Differences between previously contacted and non-previously-contacted customers.
* Customer and campaign characteristics associated with higher or lower subscription rates.

These findings can help support future campaign targeting and marketing analysis.

---

## 📌 Recommendations

Based on the analysis, businesses can further investigate:

* Customer segments showing stronger campaign response.
* Contact methods associated with better campaign outcomes.
* Appropriate campaign contact frequency.
* Call duration patterns associated with successful conversions.
* Previous campaign response patterns.
* Customer characteristics that can be useful for future campaign segmentation.

The recommendations are based on patterns observed in the analyzed dataset and should be validated with additional campaign data before being used for operational decisions.
